Re2Buy Account Deletion

Re2Buy users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to re2buyall@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Re2Buy Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered mobile number or account email address.
  4. Our team may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete your account and associated personal data that is not required to be retained by law.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain transaction records, payment records, or information required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes may be retained for the period required by applicable law.

Processing Time

We will review your request and inform you if additional information is required. Any applicable retention period will depend on legal obligations and the type of information involved.
