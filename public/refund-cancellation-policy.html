Refund & Cancellation Policy – Re2Buy

Last Updated: 28 October 2026

1. Overview

At Re2Buy, we strive to make buying and selling smooth and trustworthy. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines when refunds are permitted, how cancellations work, and what steps users should take.

2. Applicability

This policy applies to purchases, subscriptions, services, and payments made through the Re2Buy platform, including its mobile application and website, where applicable.

3. Cancellation of Services

Users may request cancellation of a service or order before it has been confirmed by the service provider or Re2Buy team, subject to the applicable service terms. Cancellation requests may be submitted through the app, website, or customer support.

4. Refund Eligibility

Refunds will be reviewed in circumstances such as:

  • A paid service is not provided by Re2Buy or its partner.
  • An incorrect or duplicate charge has occurred.
  • A cancellation qualifies for a refund under the applicable service terms.

5. Refund Process

Refund requests should be submitted using the contact details provided below or through the in-app support feature, if available. Our team will review the request and aim to respond within 7–10 business days. If a refund is approved, it will be processed using the original payment method where possible. The time taken for the funds to appear may depend on the payment provider.

6. Cases Where Refunds May Not Be Available

Refunds may not be available in circumstances such as:

  • The service has already been fully provided or completed.
  • A cancellation request is made after service delivery, where the applicable terms do not allow a refund.
  • A dispute concerns a third-party payment provider and is outside Re2Buy's control, subject to applicable law and the facts of the case.

7. Chargebacks

If a user initiates a chargeback through their bank or payment service, Re2Buy may provide relevant transaction records to the payment provider to respond to the dispute.

8. Changes to This Policy

Re2Buy may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy periodically. Updated terms will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Any changes will be subject to applicable law.
